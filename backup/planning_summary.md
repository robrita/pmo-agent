## Resource Utilization & Capacity Planning

### Potential Resource Constraints
1. **UI/UX Designer:** Not available in current resource pool for E-commerce Website
2. **Security Specialist:** Not available for Video Conferencing Application
3. **Limited QA resources during peak periods:** Some QA testers are allocated across multiple projects with overlapping timelines

### Mitigation Strategies
1. **External Hiring Plan:**
   - UI/UX Designer (2 positions) - One for E-commerce project (April-May) and one for Mobile App project (May-June)
   - Security Specialist - For Video Conferencing Application (July-August)
   - Consider additional QA resource to support during peak testing periods (July-September)
2. **Knowledge Transfer:** Implement formal handover processes for resources transitioning between projects
3. **Cross-Training:** Identify resources with potential for skill development across multiple domains
4. **Timeline Adjustments:** Consider slight adjustments to project timelines to alleviate resource bottlenecks

## Risk Assessment

### Resource-Related Risks
1. **Resource Overallocation:** Some team members are scheduled to transition between projects with minimal buffer
2. **Specialist Knowledge Gaps:** Lack of dedicated UI/UX and Security specialists may impact quality
3. **Project Timeline Overlaps:** Multiple projects running in parallel may strain shared resources

### Risk Mitigation Actions
1. **Weekly Capacity Reviews:** Monitor resource utilization and adjust allocations as needed
2. **Cross-Project Knowledge Sharing:** Implement weekly knowledge sharing sessions across project teams
3. **Contingency Planning:** Maintain a list of pre-vetted contractors who can be engaged on short notice
4. **Clear Handover Procedures:** Establish formal documentation and transition processes between projects
